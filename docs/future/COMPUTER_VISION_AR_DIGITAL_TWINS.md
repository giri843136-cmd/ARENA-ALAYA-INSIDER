# Computer Vision, AR & Digital Twins (Phase 15)

## Computer Vision
- Advanced object, brand, style, color, and room recognition.
- Fine-tuned models on ALAYA's high-quality editorial imagery + user-uploaded photos.
- Generates rich visual embeddings that feed the Recommendation Graph, Taste Graph, and Visual Search.
- Real-time analysis in the mobile app and future spatial interfaces.

## AR Experiences (Vision Pro + Future Devices)
- Place products in your actual space with accurate lighting/materials.
- "Try this collection in my living room" — spatial recommendations.
- Interactive guides: "How this linen would look and feel over the seasons."
- Shared AR collections with friends/family for gifting.
- Preparation for full spatial computing: ALAYA as a layer over your physical world.

## Digital Twins
Every important entity gets a living digital twin:

- **User Taste Twin**: A simulation of the user that can be queried ("What would Elena think of this new brand?").
- **Brand Twin**: Deep model of a brand's values, evolution, product language.
- **Collection Twin**: How a collection would evolve, what it "wants" to become.
- **Product Twin**: Not just specs, but emotional resonance, aging characteristics, pairing potential.
- **Trend Twin**: How a trend is likely to evolve and mature.
- **Lifestyle Twin**: Simulation of the user's broader life context.

These twins power:
- "What if" simulations
- Long-term planning
- Agentic research ("Run a 5-year simulation of adding this brand to my core collection")
- Better explanations ("This piece would age in a way that aligns with your preference for things that develop character")

Twins are built from the rich graphs + historical data + continuous user feedback.
