# AI Governance for Autonomous Systems (Phase 15)

When systems become agentic and autonomous, governance becomes existential.

## Core Principles
- **Human Sovereignty**: The user is always ultimately in control. The AI proposes; the human disposes.
- **Radical Explainability**: Every autonomous action or strong recommendation must come with clear, human-readable reasoning and evidence.
- **Cost & Resource Awareness**: Agents are budgeted. Expensive research or purchases require justification.
- **Safety & Values Alignment**: Nothing that violates ALAYA's editorial standards or the user's explicit preferences is executed.
- **Auditability**: Every decision, every piece of research, every action is logged and reviewable.
- **Graceful Degradation**: When in doubt, the system falls back to conservative, human-curated recommendations.

## Mechanisms
- Tiered autonomy (as described in Multi-Agent System doc).
- Explicit approval workflows for Tier 2+ actions.
- "Why did you do that?" button on every autonomous action.
- Regular "Memory & Taste Review" sessions where the user can audit and correct their graphs.
- Red-team testing of agents.
- Kill switches at user, account, and global levels.
- Full integration with Phase 10 analytics for cost and quality monitoring of autonomous behavior.

## Recovery
- Agents can be rolled back to previous versions.
- User can rewind their personal graphs to any point in time.
- Full event replay for any autonomous workflow.
- Human editors can intervene in any user's concierge at the user's request.

Governance is not a tax on intelligence — it is what makes the intelligence trustworthy enough to hand over real agency.
